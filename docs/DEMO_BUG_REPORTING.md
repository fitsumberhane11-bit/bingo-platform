# Reporting a Problem — Ethiopia Bingo (DEMO)

Found something that seems wrong? Thank you — this is exactly what a
play-test is for. You don't need to be technical to report a problem.

## Where to send it

This DEMO doesn't yet have an in-app "Help" button (you may notice it
listed but grayed out with a "Soon" label — that's a planned feature, not
a bug). For now, send your report directly to whoever invited you to this
play-test, through however they told you to reach them (chat, email,
etc.).

## What to include

Copy this template and fill it in — even a partial answer is helpful:

```
Problem:
What were you trying to do?
What happened?
What did you expect?
Device:
Browser:
Approximate time:
Screenshot/video:
Steps to reproduce:
```

**A filled-in example:**

```
Problem: My ticket count didn't update after I bought a second ticket.
What were you trying to do? Buy a second ticket for "Friday Night Bingo."
What happened? The page still showed "1 ticket" after I paid.
What did you expect? It should show "2 tickets."
Device: iPhone 13
Browser: Safari
Approximate time: around 3:40 PM
Screenshot/video: attached
Steps to reproduce: Bought 1 ticket, then bought 1 more from the same
  screen without reloading. Count stayed at 1 until I refreshed the page.
```

## Tips that help a lot

- **Screenshots or a short screen recording** are worth more than a
  paragraph of description — if you can capture the moment something
  looks wrong, do it.
- **The exact time** matters — it helps match your report to what the
  system actually did.
- **Don't worry about being "too detailed"** — more context is always
  better than less. If you're not sure whether something is a real bug or
  just confusing, report it anyway; both are useful.
- If your DEMO balance or a game result looks wrong, mention your
  **username** so it can be checked directly.

## What NOT to worry about reporting

- This is DEMO money — if a balance looks off, that's exactly the kind of
  thing to report, not something to worry about losing.
- There's no such thing as a "dumb" report here. If something confused
  you, it will probably confuse someone else too — that's valuable
  feedback on its own, even if nothing is technically broken.
