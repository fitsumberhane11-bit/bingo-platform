# Player Guide — Ethiopia Bingo (DEMO)

This is a **DEMO version** of Ethiopia Bingo. Everywhere you see money in
this app — your balance, ticket prices, prizes — it's **DEMO money only**.
No real money is ever involved. You'll see a yellow banner reminding you
of this on every page.

## 1. How to open the application

Open the link your host gave you in any web browser — phone, tablet, or
computer. No app to install.

## 2. How to register

1. Tap **Sign up** in the top right.
2. Fill in your name, a username, an email, and an Ethiopian phone number
   (e.g. `+251912345678`).
3. Choose a password (at least 10 characters, with an uppercase letter, a
   lowercase letter, a number, and a symbol).
4. Check the box confirming you're 18+ and agree to the terms.
5. Tap **Create account**.

You don't need to verify your email to start playing — this is a DEMO, so
that step is skipped.

## 3. How to log in

Tap **Log in**, enter your username (or email/phone) and password.

## 4. How DEMO money works

Every new account starts with **ETB 0** DEMO balance. To play, add some:

1. Open **Wallet** → **Deposit**.
2. Enter an amount (between ETB 20 and ETB 50,000).
3. The only available method is **Add DEMO Balance** — the real payment
   options (Telebirr, CBE, etc.) are marked "Coming soon" because this is
   a demo. Tap it.
4. Tap **Deposit**. Your balance updates instantly — no real payment step,
   no waiting.

This DEMO balance is what you use to buy tickets and what you win prizes
into. It is not real money and cannot be withdrawn as real money.

## 5. How to browse games

Open **Play Bingo** from the sidebar (or the bottom nav on mobile). You'll
see three sections:

- **Live Now** — games in progress or about to start. Tap to watch or join.
- **Upcoming** — games open for tickets, or scheduled for later.
- **Recently Completed** — past games and their results.

Each game card shows the ticket price, how many players have joined, the
current prize pool, and the winning pattern.

## 6. How to buy a DEMO ticket

1. Open a game that says **Open for tickets** (or tap **Buy Ticket**
   directly from the lobby card).
2. Choose how many tickets you want (1 is fine to start).
3. Tap **Buy 1 ticket**. Your DEMO balance debits immediately and your
   bingo card appears.

You can buy more than one ticket per game if you want more than one card
in play — there's a per-player limit shown on the page.

## 7. How to enter a game

Once you have a ticket, just stay on the game's page (or come back to it
from **Play Bingo** → **Live Now**). If the game hasn't started yet,
you'll see a countdown once the operator starts it.

## 8. How numbers are called

Once a game goes **LIVE**, numbers are called automatically every few
seconds. You'll see:

- The **current number** in large text at the top — impossible to miss.
- A strip of **recent calls** just below it.
- A full reference board further down, showing every number 1–75 and
  whether it's been called yet.

Everything updates in real time — you never need to refresh the page.

## 9. How to manually mark a number

If the game is in **manual mark** mode (shown near the game's title), tap
any number on your card once it's been called to "dab" it. A called
number that hasn't been marked yet shows a ring around it; once you tap
it, it fills in solid.

You can't mark a number that hasn't been called yet — those cells simply
don't respond to taps.

## 10. How auto-mark works

If the game is in **auto mark** mode, you don't need to do anything —
your card marks itself the instant a number on it is called. There's
nothing to tap in this mode.

## 11. How to recognize a winning pattern

Each game shows its winning pattern near the top (for example, "Full
House (Blackout)" means every number on your card must be called, or "One
Horizontal Line" means any single row). Marked/called numbers are the
ones that count toward it — the server checks for a win after every
single number call, automatically.

## 12. How winners are announced

The moment someone wins, everyone in the game sees it immediately:

- If it's **your** ticket: a gold "You won!" banner with your prize
  amount.
- If someone else won: a banner naming who won and their prize — you'll
  never see another player's card or wallet, just the public result.

When the game ends, a **Game Complete** summary stays on the page showing
the full results and a **Verify this game was fair** link, which explains
how the number sequence was locked in before the game started so it
couldn't be rigged.

## 13. How to view completed games

Open **Play Bingo** and scroll to **Recently Completed**, or open **My
Games** from the sidebar to see games you personally played, win or lose.

## 14. How to report a problem

See `docs/DEMO_BUG_REPORTING.md` (your host will tell you where to send
reports — there's no in-app "Help" button yet in this DEMO version).

---

That's everything. Enjoy the game — remember, it's all DEMO money, so
there's no pressure. Have fun!
